# Semmy AI

Semmy AI is an AI executive assistant that helps individuals and businesses manage tasks, emails, calendars, documents, and workflows through intelligent automation and verified information.

## Vision

Semmy AI is designed to become a reliable personal AI assistant that helps users organize their work, manage information, automate repetitive tasks, and make better use of their time.

## Core Features

- Task management
- Email management
- Calendar and appointments
- Document and note management
- AI-powered research
- Verified information
- Intelligent workflow automation
- Text and voice interaction
- Notifications and reminders
- Offline task updates and synchronization
- Secure data management

## Product Direction

Semmy AI is being developed with an Android-first approach, with future support for additional platforms and integrations.

## Planned Integrations

- Gmail
- Google Workspace
- Microsoft
- Slack
- ClickUp
- Trello
- LinkedIn
- Upwork
- Instagram

## Development Status

Semmy AI is currently in the early development stage.

The project is being developed incrementally, starting with the core assistant experience and expanding into integrations, automation, research, and productivity features.
